# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

Participants in the CopperMized project are expected to:

- Be respectful and constructive in all interactions.
- Focus feedback on the project and its code, not on people.
- Search for existing issues before opening duplicates.
- Report bugs and suggest improvements clearly and with relevant details.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory or offensive language.
- Sharing pirated content or instructions for cheating in Minecraft.
- Spamming or repeatedly posting off-topic content.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, issues, pull requests, and other contributions that violate this code of conduct. Repeated or severe violations may result in a ban from the project.

## Contact

For conduct-related concerns, contact the maintainer through the channels listed in [README.md](README.md#help).
